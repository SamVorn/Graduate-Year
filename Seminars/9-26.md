## what is absolute quantification and why does it matter in mass spectrometry
```
Absolute quantification referes to the precise measurement of the number of copies of a protein in a given sample, typically expressed as copies per cell or molar concentration
```
### Why it matters
- enables systems bilogy modeling by providing real concentration values
- critical for determinging protein stoichiometry in complexes and pathways
- supports understanding of translational efficiency when paired with transcriptomic data
- moves beyond relative quantification

## Peptide Detectability Prediction
- not all peptides froma protein are equally observable in MS
- MS based protein quantification typically relies on a few ""proteotypic peptides" those consistently detected 

## Metabolomics vs Proteomics
- metabolomics deals with diverse range of small molecules including endogenous metabolites, drugs, enviornmental compounds
- proteomics focuses on the analysis of proteins which are larger and structurally more complex than metabolites
