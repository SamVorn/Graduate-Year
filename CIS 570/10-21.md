# Hazzard
- Structural when two instructions in a pipeline are trying to access the same part of the pipeline at once (reading/writing)
- control hazzard - we dont have a sense of where we are in the pipeline and we have to fill the pipeline

- Branch delay is when you take an instruction that occurs during the branch in the code, and the compiler moves it to after the branch because the compiler is not impacted by its execution
    - whether the branch is taken or not
    - we have to perform the operation of the branch
